# Mori 1.1.0

Mori now speaks your language: the interface and every message Mori writes are available in 23 languages.

Mori remains a private, offline-first media and file browser: everything runs on your computer, with no account, no cloud, no telemetry and no network functionality. The full feature set is described in [docs/features.md](https://github.com/emagrina/mori/blob/main/docs/features.md).

## Highlights

**23 languages**
- English, Spanish, Catalan, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Bengali, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
- Everything is translated: menus, dialogs, analyses, and the messages that come from Mori's engine (errors, risk findings such as disguised executables, why a copy is suggested to keep, diagnostics).
- Plurals follow each language's grammar, and numbers, sizes and dates use its conventions.

**Choosing a language**
- Mori follows your system language by default.
- **Language…** in the sidebar's **More** menu (the `···` button), or in the command palette (⌘K / Ctrl+K), lists every language by its own name. Pick one, or go back to *System Language*.
- The change applies at once; you stay in the same folder and view.

**Right to left**
- In Arabic and Hebrew the layout is mirrored: sidebar on the right, text and arrows in reading order. On macOS, the window buttons keep their place.

## Safety and privacy

- Nothing about how Mori handles your files has changed. The language choice is one more preference in Mori's `settings.json`, listed under **Privacy & Local Data** like the others.
- Store builds can ask for a rating through the system's own prompt, only right after a completed task and never over a dialog. **The builds below aren't from a store, so they never ask and count nothing.**

| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | | SHA-256 of every file above |

Installation is unchanged from 1.0.0. The builds aren't signed with a publisher certificate yet, so each system warns once; the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/emagrina/mori/releases/tag/v1.0.0) explain how to open Mori on each platform. Verify your download with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing` (macOS, Linux) or `Get-FileHash <file> -Algorithm SHA256` (PowerShell).

## Known limitations

- The translations haven't been reviewed by native speakers yet. Corrections are welcome as issues or pull requests (`src/locales/` and `src-tauri/locales/`).
- Text Mori recorded before a language change keeps the language it was written in: integrity snapshot names, Undo History entries and analysis results from the current session.
- The confirmation words you type (DELETE, RESET) stay in English.
- Drag and drop works inside Mori only.
- No build is signed with a publisher certificate or notarized.
- Windows and Linux builds haven't been tested by hand yet, and have weaker decoder isolation than macOS.
- HEIC and PDF previews, new-drive detection and Undo for Trash are macOS features.
- Video and audio are played by the system's media engine, which runs outside Mori's sandbox. No sandbox is an absolute guarantee.
